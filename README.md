# 🚀 About Me

Hi, I'm Mahfuz Ahmed from Bangladesh.

My journey into programming began in 2018, right after completing my SSC. What started as curiosity quickly turned into a passion, and I took my first steps into the world of Android development. I spent countless hours learning, experimenting, breaking things, and building them again.

Later, I started studying Computer Science, but I eventually realized that my greatest lessons were coming from building and exploring on my own. I chose a different path and continued my journey as a self-taught developer, driven by curiosity and the desire to create useful things.

Over the years, I expanded my interests beyond Android and began exploring web technologies, APIs, automation, cloud infrastructure, and open source. I enjoy turning ideas into reality and believe that learning never stops.

For me, programming is more than writing code—it's about solving problems, creating value, and continuously improving one step at a time.

> *Started with Android development in 2018, continued through self-learning, and never stopped building.* 🚀
